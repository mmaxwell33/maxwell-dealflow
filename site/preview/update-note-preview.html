Update DealFlow: showings, offer PDFs and automatic bidding close
FIRST, UPDATE THE APP Close DealFlow right down and open it again. On iPhone swipe up and flick it away first. Nothing below shows up until you do. You do not need to reinstall it. The seller side now works the way the buyer side does. What is new, and how to use each piece. SHOWINGS Listings, pick the property, tap Log Showing. Date and time come from dropdowns, and the brokerage fills itself in from ones you have used before. Afterwards tap Feedback and record what the agent said and, separately, your own read on it. THE SELLER REPORT On the listing tap Send Showing Report and choose 3, 7 or 14 days. It writes the note from your record: how many showings, up or down on the stretch before, each one by date and brokerage, the feedback, and any offers. It lands in Approvals to read, change and approve. PRESENTATION TIME Set Dates now takes a time as well as a date. That is when you sit down with your seller, and bidding closes on it by itself. Leave the time blank if you do not want that. DROPPING OFFERS IN Download the offer, then drag the PDF onto the listing. On a phone, tap the box and choose it. It reads the amount, deposit, buyer, their agent and email, brokerage, conditions and the dates, and works out the percentage over or under asking. Check every figure against the document before saving. Nothing is recorded until you do. Drop several at once and it takes them one at a time. WHEN YOUR SELLER PICKS Tap Pick on the winning offer. The listing goes Under Contract, the deal opens in your Pipeline with a 17 task checklist, your commission is recorded, and the notes to the winning and unsuccessful agents are drafted in Approvals. DEPOSIT AND FINANCING Your sell side deal now asks whether the deposit has landed and whether the buyer's financing letter has arrived, instead of assuming. Nothing ticks off just because a date went by. Reread this any time: Settings, Profile, What's new.
