What it reads off the sheet

  • Address, MLS number, list price
  • Existing home or new build
  • Seller's direction and the offer deadline, when the sheet states one
  • Year built, square footage, style, beds and baths
  • Recent updates, heating, garage, lot, taxes, condo fee

How it is built

  • New js/mlsdrop.js, modelled on the offer reader you already have
  • Reads through the existing claude-chat edge function with a fixed JSON schema, so the key stays on the server
  • The note is assembled in the app from the facts it read, not written freehand by the model. Same sentence order every time, nothing invented
  • One migration: property_highlights, plus the file path and a read timestamp on viewings
  • The sheet itself is filed in the private deal-docs bucket, so any figure traces back to the page it came off

Where it is careful

  • A field the sheet does not state comes back empty. It does not guess
  • Nothing saves until you press Book Viewing
  • A failed read opens the form empty rather than blocking the booking
  • The note carries a short accuracy line in the email, since these figures come off a listing sheet
  • iPhone photos taken through the picker arrive as JPEG. A HEIC file sent from a Mac is rejected with a plain message

Three things I need you to decide

  1. Photo or PDF, or both? A downloaded PDF reads almost perfectly. A phone photo of a printed sheet reads well when it is flat and in focus, less well at an angle. I would allow both and let you see the result before booking, which the form already does.
  2. Should the note be on by default? Two options. It fills in every time and you delete it when you do not want it, or it stays empty and you tap "Add a note about the home". I lean toward on by default, since you can see it before booking.
  3. How much detail in the note? The sample above is six facts. I can go shorter, four facts and one line, or longer with room for schools and appliances. Tell me which of the facts your buyers actually ask about and I will fix the order to match.
